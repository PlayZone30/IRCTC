# Design directions — IRCTC Feature Atlas

## Three candidate approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Signal Archive | A warm editorial research desk built from railway timetables, route stamps and archival paper. It makes a complex service estate feel documented, credible and navigable. | 0.032 |
| Monsoon Terminal | A soft, climate-led travel journal with deep indigo, rain-glass layers and luminous information markers. It feels contemplative and consumer-friendly. | 0.074 |
| Circuit Board | A compact dark operations console where services become interconnected nodes. It foregrounds systems and technology rather than travel culture. | 0.018 |

## Chosen direction: Signal Archive

**Design Movement:** Swiss information design interpreted through Indian railway wayfinding and contemporary editorial dashboards.

**Core Principles:** The site uses evidence-first hierarchy, a visible route spine rather than centered card stacks, disciplined typographic contrast, and purposeful annotation. Facts are separated from navigation, and every visual accent supports scanning rather than decoration.

**Color Philosophy:** A warm paper field provides clarity and a non-generic research character. **Atlas Cobalt** carries authority and wayfinding; railway saffron marks action and discovery; deep ink anchors long-form evidence; muted green separates verified supporting services from core rail functions. The contrast is chosen for direct reading, not spectacle.

**Layout Paradigm:** The page is arranged as an asymmetrical station board. A narrow vertical service spine remains on the left at desktop widths, while research stations unfold to the right in staggered bands. The hero uses a slanted signal panel and an oversized evidence count, avoiding the conventional centered SaaS hero.

**Signature Elements:** A segmented destination-board motif, a dotted route line with labelled stops, and evidence tags styled like stamped timetable annotations recur throughout the interface.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interaction should feel like moving through a well-signed station. Category filtering, source opening, chart hover states and section jumps must be direct, calm and explain their effect immediately.

**Animation:** Only brief 160–240ms opacity and transform transitions are used. The route line and station markers may reveal with a modest stagger on first load; controls respond with a small press scale. Motion is disabled or simplified under reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** `DM Serif Display` provides the research-title voice and selected pull quotes. `IBM Plex Sans` is used for body copy, controls, tables and data labels. Eyebrow labels use a letter-spaced Plex Sans medium; headings do not use generic Inter.

**Brand Essence:** A cited visual atlas of IRCTC’s passenger-facing digital ecosystem for researchers, travelers and product observers who need scope without hype. **Methodical, grounded, navigable.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are compact and declarative; CTAs describe the action; microcopy distinguishes what was observed from what was inferred. Examples: “Every service, shown in context.” and “Open the official evidence.” Generic filler such as “Welcome to our website” and “Get started today” is prohibited.

**Wordmark & Logo:** The logo mark is a bold geometric signal made from a cobalt rail arc, a saffron departure dot, and two modular route bars. It has no text and can work as both header mark and favicon; the wordmark is custom-set as `FEATURE ATLAS` with a small “IRCTC” descriptor.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Atlas Cobalt — #0B4E9A.**
